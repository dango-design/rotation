# 029 — Plan a day on Today

**Date:** Oct 13, 2026 · **Status:** Accepted · **Replaces:** where planning happens in [021](021-plan-in-the-builder.md), and the closet parts of [028](028-board-stays-open-and-says-so.md) · **Milestone:** next

## Context

Since [021](021-plan-in-the-builder.md), "Plan it" on Today sent you to the closet, where the outfit board opened beside your pieces, set to that day. [028](028-board-stays-open-and-says-so.md) made that board easier to see when you switched tabs, but planning still meant leaving the page you started on. Denise asked whether the day could be planned without leaving Today.

## Decision

**Planning a day happens on Today.** "Plan it", "Plan today's outfit", Change and Surprise me replace the day's card with the same two halves the closet uses: your pieces, with category filters, and the outfit board beside them (a sheet along the bottom on phones). It's the same board, with the same forecast, Surprise me, and Plan for Saturday (or Log as worn). Planning or cancelling shows the day again, still on Today.

- **The week strip stays above the planner.** Picking another day shows that day and keeps the planned one waiting in a bar ("Planning Saturday · 1 piece on the outfit board, not planned yet"), with **Back to the board** and **Discard**. The waiting day's tile reads "Planning".
- **The closet stays a closet while a day is being planned.** It shows the same bar with **Back to Today**, and clicking a piece opens it. "Put on the board" in a piece's details adds it to the day's outfit, and a saved outfit opened from the closet becomes that day's outfit, back on Today.
- **The tab dot follows the board:** on Today for a day being planned, on Closet for any other outfit.
- **Building an outfit with no day** (New outfit, a saved outfit, Style it) still happens beside the closet, as before.

Rediscover is hidden while planning, so the page is just the day and the board.

## Why

- **You stay where you started.** Planning the week means looking at the week; leaving it to plan one day broke that up.
- **One board, one place for each job.** A day's outfit is built on Today, and any other outfit in the closet. A click in the closet now only puts pieces on the board when that board is in the closet.
- **Nothing new to learn.** The pieces and the board look and work the same on both pages.

## Consequences

- The closet's sort menu and saved outfits aren't beside the board while planning a day. Category filters are, and saved outfits are a click away through "Use a saved outfit" on the day.
- Phone app: not changed. Its builder is already a full-screen sheet over whatever you were looking at.

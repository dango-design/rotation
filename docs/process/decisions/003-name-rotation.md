# 003 — Name the app Rotation

**Date:** Oct 6, 2026 · **Status:** Accepted

## Context

After the move to a public, multi-store app ([002](002-public-multi-store-app.md)), the working name "Staple" was dropped. It tied the product to wardrobe basics, and to the earlier Gap-specific concept. Fifteen candidates were considered, grouped by idea: capsule wardrobes (Capsule, Kapsul, Core, Uniform), wearing more of what you own (Rotation, Rewear, Worn), building outfits (Ensemble, Remix, Pairings), garment craft (Selvedge, Seam) and plain closet words (Hanger, Fit Check, Missing Piece).

## Decision

The app is called **Rotation**. The repository was renamed from `outfit-builder` to `rotation`; GitHub redirects the old links.

## Why

- "In rotation" means the clothes you actually wear. The app's job is to bring forgotten pieces back into rotation, and to add only pieces that earn a place in it.
- It works as a verb and a metric: what's in rotation, what fell out, what a new piece would add to it.
- It is short, plain and not tied to any store.

## Open

- Trademark, domain and app-store availability have not been checked yet.
